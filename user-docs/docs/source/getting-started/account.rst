=====================
Creating an account
=====================

Welcome and log in
==================

Use the **Log In** button in the top-right corner, or the **log in** link in the welcome text, to open the sign-in page.

.. figure:: /_static/images/creatingAccount/landing_page.png
   :alt: AquiLLM welcome page with central logo, welcome message, and Log In entry points
   :width: 800px
   :align: center

   Welcome page: choose Log In to continue.

Sign in
=======

That opens the sign-in page. Click on **Continue with Google**, and sign in with a .edu email address.

.. figure:: /_static/images/creatingAccount/login_page.png
   :alt: AquiLLM sign-in page with username and password fields and Continue with Google
   :width: 800px
   :align: center

   Sign in with your credentials or a supported provider.

Main app
========

After a successful sign-in you will see the main app: the menu on the left (conversations, search, collections), the chat area, and your profile in the top-right corner.

.. figure:: /_static/images/creatingAccount/home_page.png
   :alt: AquiLLM main app after sign-in, showing sidebar menu, chat area, and user profile
   :width: 800px
   :align: center

   Signed in: you can start a new conversation and open Collections from here.
