====================
Zotero integration
====================

If you use Zotero to organize papers and documents, you can connect it to AquiLLM to simplify collection creation.

Connect your account
====================

To connect your Zotero account:

1. Click on your **username** in the top-right corner.
2. Select **Manage Account**.
3. Go to **Zotero Settings** and link your account.
